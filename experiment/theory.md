Virtual reality (VR) serves as a tool to address the complex technological issues that arise in the field of architecture as well as to foster creativity.<br><br>
Augmented reality is a feature of virtual reality (VR), wherein data is presented with animation effects in the actual world through smaller devices, typically tablets or mobile phones. VR is a tool that architects can utilize throughout the entire building design process. Different levels of detail (LOD) rendering are provided by the VR in the building, enabling the architect to comprehend the arrangement and relationship of the space early in the design process. Virtual reality (VR) has a lot of potential in the architectural field, but finding time during work for architects to explore with this technology can be difficult.<br><br>
VR gives designers and architects the ability to totally alter the design process.  Currently, game systems are more likely to concentrate on producing idealized VR experiences for end users than on developing iterative applications for the design and building of architectural projects.
